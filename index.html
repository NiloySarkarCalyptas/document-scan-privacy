Document Scan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Document Scan is a document scanner for Android, made by Niloy Sarkar. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

No data is collected

The app has no internet access. Android's internet permission is removed from the app, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your phone

Your scanned pages, the text read from them, document names, folders, signatures and stamps you create, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot open this storage.

Text recognition, document detection, QR code reading and object counting all run on your phone. No image or text is sent to a server for processing.

The app does not include its data in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your documents are deleted.

Camera

The app asks for the camera permission to scan documents, QR codes and objects. Camera images are used only for that, inside the app. They are not saved unless you keep a scan.

Photos and files you import

When you import images or files, the app reads only the items you choose in Android's picker. It does not ask for access to all your photos or storage.

Sharing and exporting

A document leaves your phone only when you share or export it yourself, for example as a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text or image file. It then goes to the app you pick, such as email or a cloud drive, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Before sharing, the app can find and hide private details such as ID and card numbers.

App PIN

If you turn on the app PIN, the PIN itself is never stored. The app keeps only a salted, one-way hash of it on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app ever needs internet access, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
