Document Scan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (version 1.1)

Document Scan is a document scanner for Android, made by Niloy Sarkar (Calyptas Apps). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your documents stay on your phone; the app uses the internet only to show ads and to download its AI model.

Your documents stay on your phone

Your scanned pages, the text read from them, document names, folders, signatures and stamps you create, AI summaries and answers, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot open this storage. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Text recognition, document detection, QR code reading, object counting and the AI features all run on your phone. No page image or document text is sent to a server for processing.

The app does not include its data in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your documents are deleted.

The AI model

The AI features (summaries, questions about a document, better names and finding private details) use a language model that runs entirely on your phone. The model file, about 2.6 GB, is downloaded from Google Play only when you choose to download it. After that, the AI works without sending anything anywhere. You can delete the model at any time in Settings.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the main tabs and, at most a few times a day, a full-screen ad when you come back to the app after sharing or exporting a document. Ads are never shown on the camera, crop, editor, page, signature, AI, PIN or Settings screens.

To show and measure ads, the AdMob software in the app collects and shares with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, information about your device, and how you interact with ads, as well as crash and performance data for the ads themselves. It does not receive your documents or anything you scan. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change that choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can also reset or opt out of personalised ads with your phone's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

No account, no analytics of our own

There is no account and no sign-in. Apart from the ads described above, the app has no analytics or tracking, and the developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Camera

The app asks for the camera permission to scan documents, QR codes and objects. Camera images are used only for that, inside the app. They are not saved unless you keep a scan.

Photos and files you import

When you import images or files, the app reads only the items you choose in Android's picker. It does not ask for access to all your photos or storage.

Sharing and exporting

A document leaves your phone only when you share or export it yourself, for example as a PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text or image file. It then goes to the app you pick, such as email or a cloud drive, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Before sharing, the app can find and hide private details such as ID and card numbers.

App PIN

If you turn on the app PIN, the PIN itself is never stored. The app keeps only a salted, one-way hash of it on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
